GRC APPLICATION / CASE STUDY

GRC Sentinel

A risk score matters when someone can see what supports it, who owns the next action, and when to review the decision. I built GRC Sentinel to connect those steps in one workflow.

Private project. The source repository is private. The authenticated demo is a local test environment. This page is a public project walkthrough.

What I built

Risk and treatment

Score inherent and residual risk, compare them on 5×5 heat maps, and flag risks above the organization’s appetite. Track treatment actions, owners, and deadlines.

Evidence integrity

Attach evidence to controls, risks, and audit workpapers. Preserve file versions, verify integrity with SHA-256, and track review dates and expiry.

Audit and remediation

Follow audit workpapers, evidence requests, and findings through review. Record risk acceptance with an approver’s rationale and an expiry date.

Access and demo isolation

Use role-based access and organization selection in the authenticated workflow. The local demo uses its own SQLite database and evidence storage.

Walk through a cloud access risk

Illustrative scenario

  1. Score the risk. An overly permissive cloud role starts at 20 out of 25, based on likelihood 4 and impact 5.
  2. Record treatment and evidence. Assign an owner to narrow the role and link the updated policy and access review to the risk.
  3. Review residual exposure. Use the evidence to reassess likelihood at 2, for a residual score of 10 out of 25. Compare that score with the organization’s risk appetite.
  4. Capture the decision. Record the review outcome and any follow-up in the risk history so the next reviewer can trace the reasoning.
