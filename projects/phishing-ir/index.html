01 / RESPONSE PATH

One incident. Six phases.

Choose a phase to inspect key actions and decision gates.

01

DETECT & ANALYZE

Preserve first. Ask clearly.

Open the incident record, preserve the original message and headers, and ask what the user did without blame. Validate indicators in approved tools. Keep confirmed facts separate from assumptions.

DECISION GATEWas the message delivered, and did anyone interact with it?
  • Capture message ID, sender, recipient, URL and delivery status.
  • Record user interaction and exact time.
  • Assign an incident lead and provisional severity.

TRAINING SIMULATION

A payment change.
A credential prompt.
A narrowing window.

A finance analyst reports an urgent supplier bank-change email and admits entering a password and approving one MFA prompt. A payment is scheduled for later that day. The team must contain access, establish scope, and stop an unauthorized transfer without overstating what evidence proves.
