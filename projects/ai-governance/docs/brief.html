The company

NexaReach has 32 remote employees, 8 freelancers and 18 clients across Pakistan, the UK and the US. AI supports campaigns, lead processing, FAQ answers and internal task preparation. The intended benefit is faster drafting and less manual work; no productivity gain is measured.

AI-01

Campaign Copilot

Draft ad copy and creative concepts.
Limit: No automatic publication; no customer lists.

AI-02

Lead Assistant

Summarize inbound requests and suggest CRM categories.
Limit: No sensitive profiling, ranking people or autonomous outreach.

AI-03

Support Guide

Draft FAQ responses from an approved knowledge base.
Limit: Draft answers only; no autonomous replies or account access.

AI-04

Workflow Assistant

Prepare internal campaign reports and task drafts.
Limit: Read-only connectors; no payments or ad-spend changes.

Three design decisions
Minimize client inputs. Independently approve public output. Keep payments and ad-spend changes outside AI authority.

Data route: remote work identity → approved workspace → minimized input → AI draft → independent review → authorized action. External text is untrusted. No live system is connected.

Fictional scenario; real project outputs. All business details and ratings are invented. Artifacts are complete and selected policy logic is simulated. Production controls, legal applicability and risk reduction remain unverified.

Bands: 1–4 Low · 5–12 Moderate · 13–19 High · 20–25 Critical.

Scoring anchors

Likelihood: 1 rare; 2 unlikely; 3 possible; 4 likely; 5 almost certain.
Impact: 1 minor rework; 2 reversible issue; 3 material harm; 4 major harm; 5 severe harm or loss of trust.
Use the highest applicable harm, not a financial loss forecast.

Release tolerance

High/Critical blocks production release. Moderate needs COO + owner acceptance with an expiry of at most 90 days. Low needs owner acceptance and review. Prohibited actions and confirmed exposure block use regardless of score.

*All target residuals are unvalidated. All 10 risks remain open. The full register records each event, rationale, affected people, control links and validation evidence. Scores prioritize work; they do not measure actual risk reduction.

04 / IMPLEMENTATION & EVIDENCE

Make the governance reviewable.

NIST AI RMF 1.0 + Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1). Selective mappings connect practical decisions to inspectable artifacts.

GOVERN

Set ownership and boundaries.

AI charter, tool approval, training, supplier criteria and escalation authority.

MAP

Understand each use case.

Four-system inventory, intended limits, input routes, affected groups and harm scenarios.

MEASURE

Test what matters.

Risk scoring, repeatable policy checks, release criteria and a monitoring specification.

MANAGE

Treat, pause and improve.

Prioritized owners and deadlines, risk acceptance rules, AI incident response and retirement.

Release evidence still needed

Live identity/settings exports, supplier terms, representative model/connector tests, privacy/rights/targeting review, stakeholder feedback and protected operational logs.

Operate and stop safely

Require every public release to be approved. A confirmed exposure or critical false claim pauses the flow. Test a 15-minute pause target, manual fallback and safe retirement before acceptance.
