## Case-study introduction
What happens when a remote marketing agency adopts AI faster than its governance? I created NexaReach Studio, a fictional agency using AI for campaigns, lead processing, FAQ support and workflow tasks. I assessed the assumed gaps, evaluated harms to clients and customers, and built an evidence-led governance package aligned to NIST AI RMF 1.0 and its Generative AI Profile.

## What I built
- Four-system AI inventory and data/use boundaries.
- Twelve findings mapped to NIST outcomes, owners and controls.
- Ten evaluated risks with rationale, treatments and unvalidated residual targets.
- Acceptable-use rules, human review, supplier criteria and a response/retirement playbook.
- A local policy simulation, generated results and a staged 90-day operational rollout.

## Honest outcome statement
Completed the project artifacts and exercised selected policy decisions using synthetic data. No real company or production AI platform was assessed. Operational control effectiveness, legal applicability and residual risk acceptance require further evidence.
