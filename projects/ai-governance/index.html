R01Client data leaks to an AI service20 · Critical+

Remote staff paste a client contact list into an AI tool; customer information is retained or exposed.

Affected: Clients and their customers.
Rating: 4 × 5 = 20; target 2 × 5 = 10 (unvalidated).

Rationale: Frequent AI use and no prompt controls make exposure plausible; personal-data impact stays severe.

Treatment: Approved enterprise tools, input minimization, restricted-data gate and retention review.

Owner: Privacy Lead · Day 45 · C02, C04, C05
Evidence needed: Verified data-flow review, DLP tests and supplier retention settings.

R02Remote AI or ad account takeover20 · Critical+

A stolen freelancer session grants access to campaign or AI workspaces.

Affected: Clients, remote workers and the agency.
Rating: 4 × 5 = 20; target 2 × 5 = 10 (unvalidated).

Rationale: Distributed identities and uneven MFA expand exposure; stolen sessions can still cause major harm.

Treatment: SSO, phishing-resistant MFA, managed devices, least privilege and rapid revocation.

Owner: IT Lead · Day 90 · C06, C12
Evidence needed: IdP/endpoint evidence and access revocation drill.

R03Prompt injection triggers unsafe actions16 · High+

Untrusted lead or web text instructs the assistant to disclose data or misuse connectors.

Affected: Customers and campaign owners.
Rating: 4 × 4 = 16; target 2 × 4 = 8 (unvalidated).

Rationale: External text is routinely processed; isolated tools reduce opportunity, not the consequence of a bypass.

Treatment: Treat external text as data, constrain connectors and gate external actions.

Owner: Engineering Lead · Day 60 · C08, C11
Evidence needed: Adversarial end-to-end model/connector tests; not demonstrated by metadata simulation.

R04Fabricated claims reach customers16 · High+

AI invents a product guarantee or policy that is published or repeated by support.

Affected: Consumers and clients.
Rating: 4 × 4 = 16; target 2 × 4 = 8 (unvalidated).

Rationale: Generative outputs can be inaccurate; reviewed source material and sign-off lower likelihood.

Treatment: Approved source material, independent fact checks, human release and escalation.

Owner: Marketing Lead · Day 60 · C07, C11
Evidence needed: Claim-verification sample and revision-bound publication approvals.

R05Unfair or intrusive audience targeting12 · Moderate+

Lead labels infer sensitive characteristics and exclude or manipulate groups.

Affected: Prospects and underrepresented groups.
Rating: 3 × 4 = 12; target 2 × 4 = 8 (unvalidated).

Rationale: Proxy attributes can bias categories; harm remains material even after review.

Treatment: Prohibit sensitive profiling; review consent, proxies and subgroup outcomes.

Owner: Marketing Lead · Day 60 · C04, C09, C11
Evidence needed: Use-case impact review with affected-user input and representative test data.

R06Supplier reuses or transfers client inputs15 · High+

An unreviewed supplier trains on prompts or stores client data in an unexpected region.

Affected: Customers and clients.
Rating: 3 × 5 = 15; target 2 × 5 = 10 (unvalidated).

Rationale: Supplier terms and subprocessors are unknown; contractual and technical checks reduce exposure.

Treatment: Review processing roles, training opt-out, locations, deletion and exit terms.

Owner: Privacy Lead · Day 45 · C05
Evidence needed: Signed terms, settings export and documented transfer assessment where applicable.

R07Unlicensed or misleading synthetic creative12 · Moderate+

Generated assets copy protected work or depict a person without permission.

Affected: Rights holders, depicted people and consumers.
Rating: 3 × 4 = 12; target 2 × 4 = 8 (unvalidated).

Rationale: Creative provenance is currently absent; documented review reduces avoidable release errors.

Treatment: Rights/provenance checklist, likeness consent and contextual disclosure.

Owner: Marketing Lead · Day 60 · C07, C10
Evidence needed: Asset rights log and documented synthetic-media release review.

R08Deepfake instruction causes payment fraud15 · High+

A synthetic executive voice persuades a remote employee to change a payee.

Affected: Agency and suppliers.
Rating: 3 × 5 = 15; target 1 × 5 = 5 (unvalidated).

Rationale: Remote requests can impersonate leaders; known-channel callback and dual approval reduce likelihood.

Treatment: AI cannot pay; independently verify payee changes and require dual authorization.

Owner: Finance Lead · Day 90 · C06, C10, C12
Evidence needed: Finance control walkthrough and callback tabletop.

R09Shadow AI bypasses governance16 · High+

Staff use personal tools outside the inventory to process client work.

Affected: Clients, workers and customers.
Rating: 4 × 4 = 16; target 2 × 4 = 8 (unvalidated).

Rationale: Tool sprawl is assumed common; training and sanctioned access reduce but do not eliminate bypass.

Treatment: Approved tools, easy request route, training and proportionate access monitoring.

Owner: GRC Lead · Day 30 · C01, C02, C03
Evidence needed: Inventory reconciliation and access/training coverage report.

R10AI outage or runaway workflow disrupts delivery12 · Moderate+

A provider failure or repeated tool loop delays support and consumes unexpected budget.

Affected: Clients and support users.
Rating: 3 × 4 = 12; target 2 × 4 = 8 (unvalidated).

Rationale: External service dependency is material; quotas and manual fallback limit frequency of disruption.

Treatment: Usage caps, circuit breaker, human fallback and tested disable/retire process.

Owner: Operations Lead · Day 90 · C08, C11, C12
Evidence needed: Quota/circuit-breaker logs, outage exercise and recovery verification.

*Target residuals are forecasts. All risks remain open until controls are deployed, tested and accepted. High/Critical scores block release. Moderate scores require COO + risk-owner acceptance for at most 90 days. Prohibited actions remain blocked regardless of score. Download the full register.
