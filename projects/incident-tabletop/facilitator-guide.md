# Facilitator guide

Status: ready-to-run original exercise; not yet facilitated with actual participants.

## Preparation

Assign incident commander, on-call analyst, cloud lead, service owner, communications lead and observer. Use synthetic data only. Confirm exercise-only channels; do not send any messages to real customers or execute containment commands. Obtain separate authorization for technical tests.

## Run of show

- 0–10 minutes: brief business scope and objectives; mark the fictional scenario clock.
- 10–60 minutes: release eight injects in order using the webpage or records.csv. The inject time is not the facilitator's measured elapsed time.
- 60–75 minutes: hotwash; participants identify assumptions and evidence gaps.
- 75–90 minutes: agree proposed owners, follow-up evidence and review dates.

## Observer rubric

For each objective record Demonstrated, Partial or Not observed; attach participant statement or decision-log ID. Objectives: ownership, containment authorization, preservation, uncertainty communication, recovery approval. Never infer technical success from a discussion.

## Debrief prompts

Which assumption most affected the decision? Which logs would distinguish possible from confirmed access? What breaks when a session survives key rotation? Who can approve a recovery while customer impact remains uncertain?

## Required outputs

Complete decision-log-template.csv and observer-template.csv with actual exercise information. The supplied timeline and model responses are scripted examples and remain unchanged. Write a new after-action report identifying actual participants and limitations.
