# Audit plan — portfolio simulation

Objective: evaluate ten original Northstar control criteria and evidence sufficiency.

Scope: October 2026 authored fixture populations plus a 5 November cutoff. No actual AWS account, vendor tenant or employees examined.

Roles: Hassan Ahmed is portfolio author and self-reviewer; no independent audit status. Business-role owners are fictional. Proposed management responses are not signed approvals.

Schedule: plan and scope; inspect fixtures; challenge initial conclusions; issue report; request corrective evidence; perform separate follow-up. No future stage is represented as completed.

Sampling: all supplied fixture records, rather than an unexplained sample selected for favorable outcomes. Small authored populations do not support statistical generalization.

Workpapers: records.json contains procedure, population, sample, result, risk, control, finding and retest. evidence-fixtures.json holds the source fixture under each E-A-xx ID. corrective-actions.json contains root-cause hypotheses and closure requirements.

Evidence custody: fixtures are synthetic and committed alongside their workpapers. A future live exercise must preserve originals privately, publish only approved redactions and record UTC time, collector, scope and hashes.

Reporting: distinguish observed exceptions from missing evidence; use internal severity, not formal certification classifications. No organization-wide compliance percentage.
